02 / The bigger idea

MATHPULSE AI

MathPulse AI is an intelligent, gamified math learning platform designed to make math practice engaging and effective. Powered by adaptive AI, it dynamically generates personalized problem sets, tracks skill mastery in real time, and turns daily practice into an interactive challenge with XP, levels, and streak rewards.

We bring together adaptive AI, pedagogy, and game design to build an experience that solves real learning challenges: instant feedback, intuitive step-by-step guidance, and measurable progress that keeps learners moving forward.

  • ✓ Adaptive Learning Support
  • ✓ Analytics & Progress Tracking
  • ✓ Student-Centered Design
  • ✓ Gamified Activity
