02 / The bigger idea

Building a clearer path through mathematics.

MathPulse AI is an educational research project exploring how artificial intelligence can support more personal, confident, and meaningful mathematics learning.

We bring together learner voice, teacher expertise, and institutional context to design a system that responds to real needs: useful feedback, accessible explanations, and insight that helps everyone move forward.

  • ✓ Adaptive learning support
  • ✓ Human-centered research
  • ✓ Actionable insights
  • ✓ Inclusive by design
